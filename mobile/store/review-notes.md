Relay is a free accessibility assistant for Deaf, hard of hearing, blind, and low-vision users. No account or login is required.

How to test without any other device:
1. Launch the app. Double tap anywhere on the purple screen (with VoiceOver on, double tap once).
2. Read and accept the data-sharing and safety screen.
3. Tap "Standalone Mode" and try:
   - Blind Mode: point the camera at the room, press and hold the bottom of the screen, ask "What is in front of me?", release. Relay speaks a short description.
   - Read Aloud: point at any printed text and tap READ.
   - Live Captions: tap START CAPTIONS and speak. Captions appear in real time. The translate button at top right (two-letter "A 文" icon) turns on translation.
   - Quick Ask (Blind Mode): tap Money, Color, Expiry date, or Sign for a one-tap answer.
   - Conversation: type a reply and it is shown upside-down for the other person and spoken in their language.
   - Settings > Name Alert: enter a name. When it is said in Live Captions, the phone vibrates and highlights it. Catch Me Up (clock icon) summarizes recent captions.
   - Braille, Quick Phrases, and Loud Sound Alert work fully offline.

Connect to Computer features (Sound Alerts, Caption Mirror, Remote Mic) need the free Relay desktop app on the same Wi-Fi network:
- Download: https://relay.suhaanthayyil.com/#download (macOS, Windows, Linux)
- In the desktop app open Settings > Phone and scan the QR code from the phone (Connect to Computer > Scan pairing code).
- Demo video of the paired features: <ADD DEMO VIDEO LINK>

Permissions:
- Camera: Blind Mode, Read Aloud, pairing QR scan. Photos are sent to OpenAI only when the user asks.
- Microphone: captions, voice questions, Remote Mic, loud sound alerts. Audio is streamed to Deepgram only while captions or the talk button are active.
- Local network: talking to the user's own Relay desktop app.

AI content: descriptions and translations are generated by OpenAI models. Users can report a response from Settings > Report an AI response. The app shows a safety notice that it is an aid and does not replace a cane or guide dog.
